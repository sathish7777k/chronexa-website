Chronexa Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 19, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Chronexa handles information when you use the application.

1. Information We Use

Chronexa may use account information provided through Firebase Authentication, such as your email address and user identifier, to authenticate your account.

2. Google Account and Calendar Access

If you choose to connect Google Calendar, Chronexa requests permission to access Google Calendar events. This access is used to support calendar-related features such as creating or updating events.

Google account access is optional and can be disconnected through your Google Account permissions.

3. How Information Is Used

Information is used to provide authentication, task management and calendar integration features. We do not sell personal information.

4. Data Security

Chronexa uses available security measures to protect authentication information and connected account access. No method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure.

5. Third-Party Services

Chronexa uses Google services for optional calendar integration and Firebase services for authentication. Their respective privacy policies also apply.
